NEXUS RETAIL INDIA - ORDER, SHIPPING & FULFILLMENT POLICY (DOMESTIC)

1. Serviceability & Pincode Coverage:
- We deliver across 19,000+ PIN codes in India through primary logistics partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, Xpressbees).
- Metro locations (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Kolkata): Delivery turnaround is 1 to 3 business days.
- Non-Metro & Tier-2/Tier-3 cities: Delivery turnaround is 4 to 7 business days.
- Remote areas (North-East, J&K, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep): Delivery takes 7 to 10 business days via India Post Speed Post.

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery Threshold:
- Standard Delivery: Free on all prepaid orders exceeding INR 799.
- Orders valued below INR 799 incur a flat shipping fee of INR 60.
- Cash on Delivery (COD): An additional handling charge of INR 50 applies to all COD orders regardless of cart total. COD is restricted to orders between INR 299 and INR 10,000.

3. Dispatch, Tracking & Cancellation:
- Same-Day Dispatch: Orders placed before 1:00 PM IST (Monday through Saturday) are dispatched the same day from our nearest regional fulfillment center (Bhiwandi, Shamshabad, or Nelamangala).
- Live tracking links are sent via WhatsApp and SMS within 2 hours of shipment handover.
- Order Cancellation: Customers can cancel an order directly via the app before the status changes to "Dispatched / Out for Handover". Once handed to the courier, cancellation is not allowed; the customer must reject the parcel at delivery.

4. Delivery Attempts & Return to Origin (RTO):
- Couriers make up to 3 delivery attempts. Customers receive an OTP-based verification for every successful handover.
- If delivery fails due to incorrect address, customer unreachable, or door locked after 3 attempts, the shipment is marked as RTO (Return to Origin).
- For prepaid orders returned as RTO, a refund is processed to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days, minus an RTO shipping fee of INR 80.

5. Damaged, Tampered, or Missing Items:
- Customers must not accept packages if the tamper-evident security tape is broken or torn.
- Claims for physically damaged items or missing products must be reported within 48 hours of delivery along with an unboxing video or clear photographs.
