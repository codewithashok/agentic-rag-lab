Nexus Retail India - Shipping, Delivery & Returns Policy

1. Shipping Rates & Delivery Timelines:
- Standard delivery covers over 19,000 PIN codes across India.
- Prepaid orders with a cart value of INR 799 or more qualify for free standard shipping.
- Orders below INR 799 incur a flat standard shipping fee of INR 60.
- Standard transit takes 2 to 4 business days for tier-1 metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata).
- Regional locations and rest of India require 4 to 7 business days. Remote locations (including North East and Andaman & Nicobar) take up to 10 business days.

2. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules:
- An additional non-refundable handling fee of INR 50 applies to all Cash on Delivery orders, regardless of total cart value.
- COD is capped at a maximum order value of INR 10,000 per order.

3. Dispatch Cut-Off:
- Orders placed before 1:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched on the same day.
- Orders placed after 1:00 PM or on weekends/public holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

4. Delivery Attempts & Return to Origin (RTO):
- Our logistics partners make up to 3 delivery attempts before classifying a package as undeliverable.
- If delivery fails after 3 attempts or the customer refuses acceptance, the package is flagged as Return to Origin (RTO).
- For orders returned to origin due to customer unavailability or refusal, a restocking and return fee of INR 80 is deducted from any refund amount.

5. Damaged or Tampered Shipments:
- Customers must inspect the outer packaging at delivery. If outer packaging is severely torn or damaged, customers should refuse delivery.
- Any claims for physically damaged contents or missing items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery along with photographic or video evidence.

6. Order Cancellations:
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge only before they are marked as "Dispatched" in our system.
- Once dispatched or marked "Out for Delivery", orders cannot be cancelled mid-transit.

7. Returns & Replacement Policy:
- Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging with tags intact.
- Items used for events, altered, or damaged by the customer are strictly non-returnable.
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days of passing warehouse quality checks.
